How to Calculate Deck Baluster Spacing?

Calculating the exact equal spacing between deck balusters (or spindles) can be a frustrating mathematical challenge for DIY woodworkers and homeowners. If you miscalculate, your railing will look uneven, and worse, it might violate local building codes.

The US 4-Inch Sphere Rule

According to the US International Residential Code (IRC), deck railing gaps must be small enough that a 4-inch sphere cannot pass through them. This is a crucial safety measure to protect small children. Our Visual Baluster Spacing Calculator automatically factors in this rule. By defaulting to a maximum target gap of 3 7/8 inches (3.875"), it guarantees your gaps are perfectly uniform and safely under the legal limit.

The Math Formula for Equal Spacing

To manually find the perfect gap, you must use this formula:

  • Take the total inside length of your rail section.
  • Divide it by an estimated gap plus the baluster width to find the number of balusters.
  • Multiply the number of balusters by their width to get the total wood space.
  • Subtract the total wood space from the total rail length.
  • Divide the remaining space by the number of gaps (which is always Number of Balusters + 1).
