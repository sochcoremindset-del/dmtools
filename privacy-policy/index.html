Privacy Policy

At Sochcore Mindset Tools, accessible via our GitHub Pages hub, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by our platform and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Operations & Data Safety

All our utilities and calculators run 100% on client-side JavaScript. We do not maintain external servers, databases, or cloud storage. Any input data or metrics you enter into our tools stay strictly inside your device's browser and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

We intend to display Google AdSense advertisements to support our platform. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site based on your visit to this and other websites on the internet. Users may opt-out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy.

3. Log Files

Like standard websites, GitHub Pages may automatically log basic information such as IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISPs), date/time stamps, and referring pages. This data is non-personally identifiable and used solely for monitoring site security and analytical trends.
